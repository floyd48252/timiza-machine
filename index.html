About Us

TIMIZA AGRO MACHINERY provides farm and agro-processing equipment to support farming and small business growth.

We offer selected products under cash and loan plans, with WhatsApp ordering for fast customer service. Our main service areas are Nakuru and Naivasha.

Why Choose Timiza Agro Machinery?

At Timiza Agro Machinery, we are committed to delivering quality agro-processing equipment that empowers farmers and agribusiness entrepreneurs across the country. With years of experience in the agricultural machinery sector, we have established ourselves as a trusted partner for farming communities in Nakuru, Naivasha, and beyond.

Our Reliability & Commitment

Reliable Quality: We source premium agro-machinery that meets industry standards, ensuring durability and long-term performance on your farm or business.

Nationwide Availability: While based in Nakuru and Naivasha, we are committed to reaching customers across the country. We work with reliable logistics partners to ensure timely delivery of equipment to your location.

Better Goods, Better Prices: We pride ourselves on offering superior quality machinery at competitive prices, making modern agricultural technology accessible to all farming levels.

Flexible Payment Options

Understanding the financial challenges of farming, we offer both cash and flexible loan plans to make quality equipment affordable. Whether you prefer immediate payment or convenient monthly installments, we have a plan that works for your business.

Customer-Centric Service

Our team is available via WhatsApp for quick inquiries, product orders, and customer support. We believe in building lasting relationships with our customers by providing responsive and dependable service.

Our Credentials

Timiza Agro Machinery is a trusted and certified provider of agricultural machinery. Our commitment to quality and excellence is reflected in our industry certifications and recognitions. We meet and exceed industry standards to ensure that every piece of equipment we provide is reliable, durable, and built to last.

Our certified status demonstrates our dedication to maintaining the highest standards of quality, safety, and customer satisfaction in the agro-machinery industry.
